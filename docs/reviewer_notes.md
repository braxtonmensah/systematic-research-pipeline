# Reviewer Notes

This repo is meant to be read as a portfolio case study, not as a trading strategy.

## What To Notice

- The workflow blocks candidates before promotion when self-correlation or crowding is too high.
- The validation report uses formula-family holdouts instead of only random splits.
- The memo explains the binding gates instead of only showing a ranked list.
- The sample data is synthetic, but the columns mirror a real research process: edge, risk, novelty, feasibility, and duplication pressure.

## What This Shows About My Work

I can build research systems that do more than generate ideas. The workflow has controls, decision logs, validation artifacts, and reviewable outputs. That is the part that transfers to deal sourcing, market mapping, diligence research, and investment workflows.

## Public Scope

The repo intentionally avoids private formulas, platform automation, credentials, and raw research state. It is a public-safe demonstration of process design.
